What this result shows

These numbers describe one unit of this chip, tested on the dates above. A clean result means no silent error showed up in these runs under these checks. It cannot rule out a fault that appears under other workloads, temperatures or ages, and another unit of the same model can behave differently. What a PASS means explains the limits in full.
