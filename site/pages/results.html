Kernel probe

{{KERNEL_ROWS}}
DeviceDateKernelsPrecisionsRunsWorst error / boundSilent faultsInjected caughtVerdictFiles

Worst error over bound is the largest measured error on the device divided by the bound at that element, across every kernel and precision. Values near 1 at fp16 and bf16 are expected: there the bound is dominated by the single rounding of the output, so there is almost nothing to spare. A value above 1 is a failed run. On NVIDIA GPUs the row also names the fused attention kernel that ran in each precision: flash, cuDNN or memory-efficient.

Checksum probe

{{CHECKSUM_ROWS}}
DeviceDateShapesPrecisionsRunsSilent faultsBlind-spot runsThreshold / typical valueInjected locatedVerdictFiles

Threshold over typical value is how large an error must be, compared with a typical element of C, before a proven checksum is guaranteed to see it, at the worst precision and shape. It is set by the shape, the precision and the arithmetic model, not by the chip's answers, because the threshold adds up the worst-case rounding of every element in a row: on the H100 it runs from 2.3 (fp32 at 1024×1024×1024) to 827 (bf16 at 32×4096×11008). Below it, the reference and repeat checks carry the smaller faults. Blind-spot runs are runs the reference check failed while every checksum held.

Hours under load probe

{{SOAK_ROWS}}
DeviceDateLengthShape (M×K×N)PrecisionsRunsConditions during the soakSilent faultsInjected caughtVerdictFiles

Every run under load is compared bit for bit with a first result that was checked against the proven bound, so these runs are counted apart from the element-by-element compute runs above. Conditions come from the chip's own telemetry (NVML on NVIDIA GPUs): the temperature, SM clock and power range across the whole soak, and the reasons the driver gave for running below full clock. sw-power-cap means the chip sat at its software power limit and lowered its clock to stay there, which is the state marginal silicon is most likely to fail in.

Rented data centre GPUs are young, monitored and already screened, so clean rows are the expected result there. The research says faults concentrate in aged units and appear under data-dependent, long, hot workloads. The hours-under-load probe is the first step towards those conditions; longer soaks on older units are where the first real findings are expected.
